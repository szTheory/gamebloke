# v3.0 Milestone Audit — PPU Modes and Composition

## Result

All 18 v3.0 requirements are satisfied by their assigned phase verifications
and summary records. All five phase verifications are current and pass, and the
cross-phase audit found no broken connections or incomplete end-to-end flows.
The result is `tech_debt` because selected PPU policies, silicon disputes,
fixture-rights limits, and historical Phase 6 evidence carryovers remain open.

and composition. It does not establish cycle-perfect PPU timing, hardware
qualification, broad retail compatibility, rights to redistribute test
content, hosted CI, or release readiness.

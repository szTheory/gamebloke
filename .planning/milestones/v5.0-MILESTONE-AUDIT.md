## LibFuturo Assessment

The owner-provided source review was read-only against LibFuturo HEAD
`4177a43e67312e2db88ab21cba13aa7350063105` and its relevant dirty source files.
It confirms that the current public route cannot combine the Gamebloke
cycle-1536 input change with an independent caller-selected 2,048-cycle ceiling
in one advance. GabbaBoy applies an adapter-selected frame bound, not the
caller's cycle ceiling. Video and audio conversions appear bridgeable, but
preflight refusal guarantees do not establish rollback after native execution
starts; an output-full stop can map to `LF_BUFFER_TOO_SMALL` after work began.
No public GBA/AGB profile, built/installed LibFuturo artifact, hardware
qualification, or adoption approval was established. The recommendation remains
to keep Gamebloke v1 as the only demonstrated GBA consumer route and defer
adoption or contract changes.

## User story

**As** the owner/developer, **I want** every fixture declared in a linted manifest row with its source, pinned revision, license, build recipe, toolchain, BIOS requirement, provenance class, and permitted use, **so that** no fixture can enter the evidence chain unpinned or with an unstated provenance. (Phase story: see 02-01-PLAN.md; decision index there.)

Decisions implemented: D-01, D-02, D-04, D-12, D-13, D-14, D-15. Orchestrator resolutions R-3, R-4.

Reads contract, registry, manifest (each failing on its own codes), loads the result, and reports family `result`, every finding before finishing:
- `missing-field`: schema, fixture, fixture.id, status, claim, disputes, labels, limits absent or null; `fixture.ran` not BOOLEAN; `fixture.rom_sha256` key absent or neither STRING nor NULL; any claim field from `<vocab>_CLAIM_FIELDS` absent (detail is the field path).
- `bad-schema`: not exactly `gamebloke.result/1`.
- `unknown-status`: status not in `<vocab>_STATES` (exact case).
- State rules from the contract row (never hard-coded): for PASS, ran OFF is `pass-without-run` and a rom_sha256 that is not 64 lowercase hex is `pass-without-digest`; for other states, a ran value contradicting a `true` or `false` row is `ran-state-mismatch` (booleans read as ON/OFF after TYPE BOOLEAN), and a required digest that is null or malformed (or an optional digest that is a malformed string) is `missing-digest`; a required reason that is absent or empty is `missing-reason`; a required refusal lacking a non-empty `refusal.code` or `refusal.postcondition` is `missing-refusal`.
- Claim: `bad-machine` (not `agb`), `bad-boot` (not `direct-start`), `bad-bios-provider` (gbl_bios_provider_valid FALSE), `bad-cart` (not an object, kind not `rom-only`, or rom_sha256 neither null nor 64 lowercase hex), `bad-input-timeline` (kind not `none` or `script`, or `script` without a 64-hex `sha256`), `bad-evidence` (not an array, empty, an item missing `fixture` or `provenance_class`, a class outside `<vocab>_CLASSES`, or a class differing from that fixture's manifest row class), `claim-mismatch` (claim.cart.rom_sha256 differs from fixture.rom_sha256, both nulls being equal; or no evidence item names fixture.id).
- References: `unknown-fixture` (fixture.id or an evidence fixture absent from the manifest ids); `unknown-dispute` (a `disputes` item not in the registry ids or not a non-empty string); `undisclosed-dispute` (a manifest row for fixture.id or an evidence fixture has a dispute that is missing from `disputes`, or `disputes` is non-empty while `labels` lacks `disputed`); `label-without-dispute` (`labels` contains `disputed` while `disputes` is empty); `bad-label` (a label other than `disputed`, or a duplicate label; `disputed` is hard-coded here on purpose, I2: its meaning is the disclosure rule above, so it is not read from contracts/evidence.json, and a new label would arrive with its rule and a `gamebloke.result/2` bump; say so in the header comment).
- `empty-limits`: limits not an array, empty, or holding an item that is not a non-empty string.

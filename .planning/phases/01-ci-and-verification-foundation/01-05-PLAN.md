scripts/linux-lane.sh (D-02 POSIX sh; developer tool, not called by CI): usage `linux-lane.sh <workflow-preset> [gcc|clang]` (default compiler: clang for `asan`, gcc otherwise); anything else prints `linux-lane: usage` and exits 2 before touching Docker. Run `docker run --rm -v "$PWD":/src:ro -w /work <image> sh -c '...'` where the inner script: `apt-get update`, installs `cmake ninja-build git` plus `gcc` or `clang-21 libclang-rt-21-dev`, copies /src to /work with `cp -R /src/. /work`, removes /work/build, runs `git config --global --add safe.directory /work`, exports `GBL_EXPECT_C_ID` (`GNU` or `Clang`) and `CC` (`gcc` or `clang-21`), prints `cmake --version`, then runs `cmake --workflow --preset <preset>`.

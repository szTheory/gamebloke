Rules and reason codes (D-01, D-10, D-11, D-16): `workflow: moving-label: <line>` (a runs-on or matrix runner value ending in `-latest`); `workflow: path-filter` (`paths:` or `paths-ignore:` anywhere under `on:`); `workflow: pr-target`; `workflow: needs-missing: <job>` (every job id other than ci-required must appear in ci-required's needs list); `workflow: required-check` (no job named `CI required` with `if: always()`); `workflow: expression-in-run: <line>` (a `${{` on a `run:` line or inside a `run: |` block); `workflow: non-preset-run: <job>` (in jobs build, asan, hygiene, any run line that invokes cmake must be exactly `cmake --workflow --preset <name>`); `workflow: unknown-preset: <name>`; `workflow: no-timeout: <job>`; `workflow: permissions` (top-level permissions must be exactly contents read); `workflow: persist-credentials: <line>` (a checkout step without `persist-credentials: false`); `workflow: continue-on-error: <line>`. Report all findings, exit 1 if any, else `workflow: ok (<n> jobs)`. This gate covers the VALIDATION.md row for VERIF-01's preset-only CI and needs completeness under the single test name `ci.yml.policy`.
